Saturation is a value between -50 and 125. It is primarily increased by eating Food Items, but also by drinking some Fluids.

Saturation naturally decreases at a rate of 0.0435/s or 2.61/m, multiplied by the "Metabolism rate" run setting. While body temperature is below 32°C, saturation is additionally decreased by 0.035/s, for a total of 0.0785/s or 4.71/m.
